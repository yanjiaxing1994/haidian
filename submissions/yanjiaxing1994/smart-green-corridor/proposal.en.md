# Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt Urban Design

## Design Basis and Documentation

This formal proposal takes the *Prequalification Announcement for the International Solicitation of Urban Design Proposals for the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt* issued by the Beijing Municipal Planning and Natural Resources Commission, Haidian Branch as its primary basis, and uses the machine-readable provisional rough boundary, key areas, enumerations, indicators, and source inventory registered by maintainers in `brief/site-package/` as its computational basis.

## Three-Level Scope Framework

The proposal is organized according to three levels defined in the announcement:

1. **Strategic Research Scope (43.6 km²)**: AI industry ecosystem, strategic positioning, innovation chain, and future urban form
2. **Overall Design Scope (11.4 km²)**: Urban renewal framework, industrial spatial layout, transportation and municipal support, urban character control
3. **Key Area Scope (368.4 ha)**: Three detailed design areas requiring functional programming, building scale, retain-renovate-demolish classification, public space connectivity, and transportation organization

## Core Concept: "Jing-Zhang Smart Symbiotic Belt"

The proposal's core concept takes the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park as the historical and public space spine, with three key areas (Zhongzhi Park AI Innovation Zone, Beijing AI Origin Community, Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster) as innovation anchors, and universities, enterprises, communities, and transit stations forming the daily network.

## AI Innovation Ecosystem and Talent Profiles

The proposal establishes spatial demand profiles for AI talent and enterprises, covering R&D office, open-source collaboration, achievement display, enterprise services, talent housing, social learning, consumer life, sports and leisure, and international exchange.

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish

The land use plan follows national land spatial survey and planning classification standards. Building proposals distinguish between retain, renovate, renew, new construction, and to-be-confirmed objects.

## Transportation, Rail Transit, Municipal and Public Service Facilities

Transportation proposals address transit station integration, road micro-circulation, slow-traffic gap closure, external connections, parking, bicycle parking, and green transportation systems.

## Blue-Green Space, Public Space, and Urban Character

The blue-green space plan uses the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park vitality belt as the backbone, coordinating the Qinghe River, Xiaoyue River, and surrounding university, enterprise, and community access needs.

## Renewal Project List, Implementation Policy and Phasing

The implementation plan includes a reviewable renewal project list with project location, type, function, responsible entity, dependencies, implementation phase, risks, and evaluation indicators.

## Metrics, Area Recalculation and Compliance Matrix

The metrics system includes at minimum: overall design scope area, key area areas, green space and public space ratios, building footprints, renewal project count, AI scenario nodes, slow-traffic connectivity metrics, industrial space metrics, talent service metrics, and self-check status.

## Risk, Copyright and Compliance Statement

This proposal does not claim official approval, ratified regulatory planning, final land ownership, final construction scale, or guaranteed implementation.
